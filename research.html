Visual computing is a generic term for all computer science disciplines handling with 3D models and images, i.e. Computer Vision, Computer Graphics, Image Processing, Visualization, Virtual and Augmented Reality, Video Processing, but also includes aspects of Pattern Recognition, Human Computer Interaction, and Machine Learning. The core challenges are the acquisition, processing, analysis and rendering of visual information (mainly point clouds, meshes, images, and video). Application areas include industrial quality control, medical image processing and visualization, surveying, robotics, multimedia systems, virtual heritage, special effects in movies and television, and computer games.
